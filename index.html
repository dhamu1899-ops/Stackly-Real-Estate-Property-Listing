Who we are

A smarter, safer way to buy & sell property

Stackly began in 2011 with a simple frustration — property buying in India felt opaque. We rebuilt the experience around three promises: every listing verified in person, every rupee explained in writing, and one advisor with you from shortlist to registration.

  • Triple-checked titles — legal, encumbrance and layout approval verified before a listing goes live.
  • Honest pricing — real comparable sales data, so you never overpay at the negotiating table.
  • End-to-end paperwork — loans, registration and tax guidance handled with empanelled partners.
