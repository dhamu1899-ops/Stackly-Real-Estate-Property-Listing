Our story

Born from a frustrating, opaque property search

In 2011, our founder walked away from a deal after discovering the "verified" apartment he was buying had an unresolved encumbrance. Stackly started the next month with one rule: we verify before we list — physically, in person, on camera.

Fifteen years later that rule still decides what appears on this website. Every listing you see has been walked through by an advisor on our payroll, photographed by our own team, and priced against real transactions — not aspirational quotes.

  • 100% listings physically inspected before publication
  • One dedicated advisor from shortlist to registration
  • Written, itemised costs before any token advance

The journey

Fifteen years, one promise

2011

One storefront in Salem

Stackly opens with three advisors and a single rule: verify before you list. First sale closes in 41 days.

2014

Chennai & Coimbatore

The verification playbook travels well — two new offices, 200 verified homes on the books by year end.

2017

500th family served

Legal & loan desks become in-house. Average deal cycle drops from 94 to 62 days.

2020

4K tours & NRI desk

Lockdown pushes every site visit online. The NRI desk now serves buyers from 11 countries.

2023

The Stackly platform

Customer accounts, live enquiry tracking and an admin console bring the whole marketplace online.

2026

26 cities, 2,400+ listings

Still family owned, still visiting every property in person — now with 40+ advisors on staff.
